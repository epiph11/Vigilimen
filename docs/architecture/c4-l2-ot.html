Read this before using the zone labels

The zone partition below is provisional, and deliberately so

Zones are drawn from consequence, and consequence is assessed at ZCR 2 — which happens at Sprint 7. Anything assigned now is a working grouping to build against, not a partition. It is written down anyway because a team that waits for the formal assessment before grouping anything builds a flat network and then discovers the partition is a re-cabling exercise. Provisional and recorded beats absent and assumed, provided the word provisional is on the page.

Why the gateway is its own container

A protocol break is a design object

The gateway exists so that the thing speaking OPC UA northbound is not the thing speaking Modbus down. Collapsing them would put an internet-reachable protocol stack in the same process as the controller's client, and every later argument about conduits would be about a boundary that does not exist.

Why three containers are dashed

Drawn before built, marked as such

The SCADA, historian and engineering workstation arrive at Sprint 4. They are on this drawing because the conduits into them are already decided, and a drawing that only shows what exists cannot be reviewed before it is built. The dashes are the honesty: three of eight containers here have never run.

Why the AND is drawn in copper

The series connection is not logic

Both controllers reach the contactor, and the conjunction between them is a physical series connection — not a rung in either program. A software AND can be written around by whoever owns the software. That is the entire content of ADR-011, and FF-06 exercises it on every commit with thirteen cases.
